Questions

Before you hand over your email

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no card, no trial and no sales sequence attached. You get the PDF and the weekly essay, and you can unsubscribe from either in one click.

How long does the worksheet take to complete?

About two hours if you already have call recordings to work from, and roughly a week if you need to run the nine interview questions with five recent buyers first.

Does this work for a company that sells to consumers?

Parts of it do, but the worksheet assumes a buying committee and an internal champion, so it is built for companies selling to other businesses.

What happens to my email address?

It goes into the email platform that sends the weekly essay and nothing else. It is never sold, shared or passed to an advertising network.
